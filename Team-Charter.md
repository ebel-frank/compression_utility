## Conflict Resolution

When technical disagreements arise, we will:

1. **Discuss openly:** Present perspectives and reasoning clearly with the TA
2. **Document trade-offs:** List pros/cons of each approach
3. **Seek consensus:** Try to reach agreement through discussion
4. **Tech Lead decides:** If consensus isn't reached, the Tech Lead makes the final call
5. **Move forward:** Commit to the decision and revisit if needed after implementation
